# QuantumRisk

![QuantumRisk](web/public/art/title.webp)

A strategy game of territorial conquest on a computer mainboard, for 2–4 players in the browser.
Battles have no dice: the bigger army wins. The only uncertainty is quantum. You can put your troops
in superposition across two sectors, and nobody, not even you, knows where they are until an attack
measures them.

**Goal:** complete your secret mission (or, in Domination mode, control every sector) before your
opponents do.

The measurements come from quantum circuits run on the
[Moth Quantum platform](https://docs.mothquantum.com/docs/intro), on real quantum hardware or on
Moth's quantum emulator.
